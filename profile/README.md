# Rivento Studio

Websites and product dashboards for AI and SaaS startups, designed and built by one team.

We're a two-person studio. Baishali leads design and Sandipan leads development, and the same two people take every project from the first call to launch. Most sites are typically live in 2-3 weeks.

## What we do

- **Website design and development** in Framer, Webflow, WordPress or custom code
- **Dashboard and product UI**, design only or design and build
- **Logo and brand identity** for new products
- **Website redesign**, keeping what works and protecting your search traffic
- **Website maintenance** after launch

## How we build

Custom builds are hand-coded in React, Next.js or Astro with Tailwind. Our own site is built with Astro.
